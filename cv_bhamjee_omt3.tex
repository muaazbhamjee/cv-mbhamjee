\section{Profile}
Associate Professor and Founder / Principal Investigator of the CERG-FLUX Lab (Fluids, Learning, and Uncertainty in compleX systems) at the University of Pretoria, with a background in Mechanical Engineering and Applied Mathematics. Research spans computational fluid dynamics and multiphase / granular flow (Navier-Stokes and Lattice Boltzmann methods on high-performance computing), scientific machine learning and AI for climate, experimental high-energy physics with the ATLAS Collaboration at CERN, and hybrid quantum computing and machine learning.
